Tools

Flowerhorn tools

Four small tools that answer the questions we get asked most in the store.

具

Pick a tool

Everything here runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded, nothing needs an account, and none of it costs anything. They are built on the same guidance as our written guides, just faster to use while you are standing in front of a tank.

ToolsFlowerhorn grading scorecardScore a flowerhorn on kok, flower, pearling, color, body, eyes, fins and behavior. Weighted out of 100, with notes on what to fix first.ToolsMale or female checkerAnswer what you can see on your flowerhorn and get a weighted male or female read, with the confidence and the reasoning shown.ToolsTank and equipment calculatorWork out the tank size, filter flow, heater wattage and weekly water change one flowerhorn needs, in gallons or liters, by stage of growth.ToolsGrowth and kok trackerLog your flowerhorn's length and kok development month by month, see the growth rate, and export the record as a CSV file.

How we use them at the store

  • Scoring a fish before we price it. The scorecard keeps our judgment consistent between people and between weeks.
  • Settling the male or female question. Customers ask constantly. Walking through the signs in order is faster than arguing about koks.
  • Sizing a setup for a new keeper. The calculator turns a fish into a shopping list, so nobody goes home with a tank that is too small.
  • Tracking a grow-out. The tracker is how you find out whether a food or routine change actually did anything.

If something here is wrong or missing, tell us. These are meant to be used, not admired. You will find our stores and contact details on the about page.
